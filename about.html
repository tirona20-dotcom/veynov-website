03 / About VEYNOV

Why VEYNOV?

We work on the parts of business that rarely fit into a neat box: records, obligations, paperwork and decisions. VEYNOV is the name bringing those projects together.

Where things standVEYNOV is a developing identity. Its projects are not being presented as finished, commercially available products.
THE REASON FOR THE WORK

People shouldn't have to spend their best attention chasing paperwork or checking whether they've missed a detail.

01 / What matters to us

Take the work seriously. Keep the tool simple.

There's no value in making a task look modern if it becomes harder to finish.

01 / CARE

Pay attention to the details.

A date, a missed attachment or an old version can change the whole picture.

02 / RESTRAINT

Don't automate for show.

Sometimes a helpful reminder is worth more than another clever feature.

03 / HONESTY

We'd rather say a project is still being tested than sell an expectation as a product.
