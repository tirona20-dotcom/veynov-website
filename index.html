02 / Our approach

A better tool begins with the work itself.

A polished interface means little if the information behind it can't be trusted.

01 — CHECK

Begin with the source.

The date, author and version of a document can matter just as much as the words on the page.

02 — RESPECT TIME

Make routine work easier.

Good software shouldn't add five new steps to a task that already takes too long.

03 — LEAVE ROOM

Leave room for judgement.

Not every decision can, or should, be handed over to a machine.
